### 2. Limits of Statistical Assurance for Embodied and Agentic AI under Adaptive Adversaries

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18948)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`certification limits`、`adaptive adversary`、`post-certification attack`、`theory`

👤 **作者**：Inderjeet Singh

- 🎯 **研究动机**：有限安全评估无法排除在认证前保持未攻击行为、认证后再激活的对手——该限制对具身与工具型 AI 的刻画缺失
- 🔬 **研究方法**：理论刻画（认证时刻切换策略的期望事后事件数 v·E[(n-S0)+]，覆盖自适应与私有随机化评估器）+RoCo 仿真与预注册 AgentDojo 试验+对抗语言模型控制器从噪声评估回执推断隐藏认证
- 📌 **结论**：RoCo 34/36 认证后回合出现 commanded-separation 违规；AgentDojo 每四任务延续 +1.98 次违规；永久撤销下混合似然比监视器以平方根阶累积超额——认证-激活式攻击的统计保证极限

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Finite safety assessments cannot exclude an adversary that preserves unattacked behavior until certification and activates afterward. We characterize this limitation for embodied and tool-use AI. An adversary that matches the complete observation law until observable, irrevocable certification and retains intervention access afterward attains $v\mathbb{E}[(n-S_0)_+]$ expected post-certification events over $n$ trials, where $v$ is the active event probability and $S_0$ the unattacked certification time. The result covers adaptive and privately randomized evaluators. Switching at certification is exactly optimal for a fixed likelihood-ratio test under memoryless, event-increasing interventions without a binding intervention budget. A positive clean-certification requirement forces linear worst-case expected excess when intervention raises event rates over an authorized deployment horizon, even if certification is deferred to the deadline. In simulated RoCo collaboration, switching yields commanded-separation violations in 34 of 36 post-certification episodes. Preregistered randomized AgentDojo trials estimate 1.98 additional security violations per four-task continuation (95% interval [1.67, 2.29]). An adversarial language-model controller infers hidden certification from noisy evaluation receipts and times intervention accordingly. With permanent revocation, a mixture likelihood-ratio monitor bounds cumulative excess above a prespecified reference rate at square-root order, up to logarithmic factors, on every sample path. A single fixed alternative can permit linear expected excess under stationary attacks. Against a controller observing the monitor's rule and statistic, live mixture monitoring reduces violations from 12.00 to 3.81 per 24 assigned task opportunities. Revocation also reduces tasks executed.

</details>

### 3. CyberArena: Benchmarking AI Agents in Live Symmetric Attack-and-Defense Combat

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19005)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`AI-for-cyber`、`attack-and-defense`、`symmetric combat`、`benchmark`

👤 **作者**：Bangya Liu、…、Dawn Song

- 🎯 **研究动机**：现实网络行动是动态耦合的：攻守实时相互适应、安全态势持续变化——静态基准快速饱和且测不出同时攻防的实时压力
- 🔬 **研究方法**：CyberArena：隔离沙箱内两 agent 同规则对称竞技（持续变化的服务上并发攻防）+自动 agentic 管线持续生成/审查/验证新环境+把对局动态映射为攻/防/领域能力多维分的动力学模型
- 📌 **结论**：12 前沿 LLM×40 挑战×466 场对局区分了单智能体基准天花板之上的能力——活体对称攻防的网络基准

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

AI agents are demonstrating increasingly strong offensive and defensive cybersecurity capabilities in the real world. Current benchmarks mostly evaluate agents in isolation against static targets. Real cyber operations, however, are dynamic and coupled: security postures constantly change as attackers and defenders adapt to each other in real time. Static benchmarks quickly saturate and miss an agent's ability to attack and defend simultaneously under live pressure. To address this gap, we present CyberArena, a dynamic, symmetric adversarial benchmark built around multi-round attack-and-defense combat. In isolated sandboxes, two agents compete under identical rules, acting concurrently as both attackers and defenders over continuously changing services. This converts static targets into evolving, semi-open challenges, directly testing an agent's speed and precision in discovering, exploiting, and patching vulnerabilities. To sustain the benchmark's longevity, an automated agentic pipeline continuously generates, reviews, and validates new environments. Furthermore, because pairwise contests are notoriously hard to quantify, we introduce a dynamical model that maps match dynamics into unified, multidimensional scores across offensive, defensive, and domain-specific capabilities. Evaluating 12 frontier LLM agents across 40 challenges in 466 matches, CyberArena separates model capabilities where single-agent baselines hit a ceiling. The trajectories read like a fight rather than an exam: defenders patch one hole and miss the next and certify repairs on stale evidence, while capable agents think a move ahead, planting backdoors for later rounds and, in one case, writing their own packet sniffer to watch the opponent. Beyond a benchmark, CyberArena serves as an adaptable, scalable experimental platform for future research on cybersecurity agents.

</details>

### 4. TRACER: Trace-Regularized Training Against Indirect Prompt Injection

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19133)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`indirect prompt injection`、`adversarial training`、`channel-aware`、`defense`

👤 **作者**：Kshitij Mishra、Salem Lahlou、Nils Lukas

- 🎯 **研究动机**：同一注入作为后续消息成功 18%、放进检索上下文成功 53%——防御鲁棒性是信道条件的，而现有训练不区分部署信道
- 🔬 **研究方法**：TRACER：信道感知对抗训练（采样呈现包装器）+答案锚定证据门控奖励+ATRC 在注入无效时把被攻推理对齐干净证据
- 📌 **结论**：GSM8K 上 RAG 框架 ASR 65.5%→6.1% 保 78-82% 干净精度；7B 单 epoch 达 9.4%——注入防御须跨信道显式训练与评测

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Indirect prompt injection uses untrusted retrieved content, tool outputs, or agent messages to override an LLM's intended behavior. We show that defense robustness is channel-conditioned: the same attack succeeds 18% of the time as a follow-up message but 53% when placed in retrieved context. We propose TRACER (Trace-Regularized Adversarial Channel Exposure with Reward-shaping), a channel-aware adversarial training method that samples presentation wrappers and combines an answer-anchored, evidence-gated reward with ATRC, which aligns attacked reasoning with clean evidence when an injection is invalid. On GSM8K with Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, TRACER reduces RAG-framed ASR from 65.5% to 6.1% while preserving 78-82% clean accuracy. Joint RAG+tool training achieves 13.9% average ASR across framings at 3B scale, while a single epoch at 7B reaches 9.4%. These results demonstrate that robust defenses must explicitly train and evaluate across deployment channels.

</details>

### 5. AgentCIScope: Grounded Contextual Integrity Evaluation for LLM Agents

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19158)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`contextual integrity`、`agent privacy`、`benchmark`、`disclosure constraints`

👤 **作者**：Chengquan Guo、Zhaorun Chen、Zinan Lin、Xiaodong Liu、Bo Li

- 🎯 **研究动机**：agent 跨邮件/文档/应用读写敏感数据——信息流是否遵守情境特定的披露约束（CI）是中心隐私问题，既有工作只测模型级泄漏或人工小环境
- 🔬 **研究方法**：AgentCIScope：公开与合成隐私案例转为可执行多步任务，披露约束三重接地（隐私政策/persona 协议/敏感属性分类）；12 agent×936 任务
- 📌 **结论**：强任务完成不保证 CI 保持：单次泄漏率 32.5-94.4%，重复五次再增 4-19pp；偏好与预订细节泄漏最高（84.7%/76.5%）——agent 级情境完整性平台

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

LLM agents are becoming increasingly capable of handling daily workspace tasks by interacting with application environments through tool calls. However, as agents read and write sensitive user data across email, documents, and other applications, a central privacy question is whether they preserve contextual integrity (CI), that is, whether the information flows they produce comply with context-specific disclosure constraints. Existing work either focuses on model-level privacy leakage or evaluates agents in limited application environments with manually constructed disclosure constraints. In this work, we introduce AgentCIScope, a unified platform and benchmark for evaluating agent-level contextual integrity through executable, multi-step tasks across diverse, realistic application environments. AgentCIScope transforms public and synthetic privacy cases into executable agent tasks, with disclosure constraints grounded in three criteria: governing privacy policies, persona-based agreement, and a predefined sensitive-attribute taxonomy. Leakage varies substantially across attributes: personal preferences and booking details have average leakage rates of 84.7% and 76.5%, compared with 17.1% for credit-card information and 29.4% for SSNs; environments: calendar has the highest average leakage rate as an information source, while WhatsApp ranks highest as an information destination; and governing privacy policies. AgentCIScope provides deep insights into agent privacy leakage and serves as a unified testbed for developing privacy-preserving agents.

</details>

### 6. Beyond Maliciousness: Static and Runtime Attack-Surface Profiling of Agent Skills

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19264)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`agent skills`、`attack surface`、`capability profiling`、`permission model`

👤 **作者**：Ratnesh Pandey、Japneet Singh、Harsh Akshit、Sonali Tyagi、Cassie Crossley

- 🎯 **研究动机**：安装一个 skill 即授予指挥 agent 的权威（会话权限）——授权规模从未被测量且安装时几乎不可见
- 🔬 **研究方法**：两阶段画像：整包静态分析+依赖出处检查+插桩沙箱执行与轨迹的模型评估；两大市场 6,309 个上架（选择不预设威胁信号）
- 📌 **结论**：半数携带 shell 执行证据、35% 文件读、31% 文件写/网络出口、13% 凭据访问，七成至少一项提权能力；frontmatter 权限模型近乎无效（770/6061 声明、无法表达凭据访问且无校验，59% 声明不准）——skill 安装即接受的隐形攻击面

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Agent skills are Markdown documents that a tool-using agent reads as instructions and then acts on with the privileges of the session that loaded them. Installing one grants authority (the standing to direct the agent, not new privilege), and the size of that grant is what we measure. We present a two-phase attack-surface profiling methodology that combines whole-bundle static analysis and dependency provenance checking with instrumented sandbox execution and separate model-based assessment of the resulting traces. We apply the methodology to 6,309 listings from two marketplaces, selecting listings for execution without conditioning that decision on a prior threat or risk signal. Half the corpus carries evidence of shell-execution capability; the corresponding shares are 35% for filesystem read, 31% each for filesystem write and network egress, and 13% for credential access; seven listings in ten carry at least one elevated capability. That is the surface a user accepts at install time, and almost none of it is visible then. A permission model exists but is close to inert. Authors may list the tools a skill pre-approves in its own frontmatter, which removes confirmation prompts rather than restricting what the skill can reach; of the 6,061 listings whose frontmatter parses, 770 declare anything at all, the vocabulary cannot express credential or environment access, and nothing checks what it does express against the bundle. Declaring is also not declaring accurately: 59% of declaring listings understate what their bundle can do. ClawHub declares roughly twice as often as skillsmp, yet under-declares on 88% of its declarations against 31%. Triage at the marketplace boundary therefore cannot rest on what a listing declares. It must rest on what the bundle can do. These results position capability-oriented attack-surface profiling as a necessary complement to maliciousness detection.

</details>

### 7. Did the LLM Answer the Question? A Semantic Reconstruction Game for Jailbreak Evaluation

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19306)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`jailbreak evaluation`、`judge validity`、`semantic reconstruction`、`ASR`

👤 **作者**：Liam Davis、Romain Emil-Popescu、Suman Jana

- 🎯 **研究动机**：越狱评测的 ASR 依赖正确识别履行了禁止请求的响应——既有评估器会把拒绝或主题相关的响应误判为攻击成功
- 🔬 **研究方法**：语义重构博弈：仅凭响应让 LLM 重构其实质内容所回答的请求（排除仅复述或拒绝的信息），与相似诱饵请求比较判定是否匹配原请求、允许弃权
- 📌 **结论**：仅换 judge LLM 可使报告 ASR 变化达 82 个百分点；博弈在人类标签上取得最低平均误差并降低 ASR 方差、优先避免假阳性——越狱评测判定的免训练修正

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Jailbreak attacks against large language models (LLMs) are evaluated using attack success rate (ASR), which depends on correctly identifying responses that fulfill prohibited requests. Existing evaluators can mistake refusals or topically related responses for successful attacks. We show that changing only the judge LLM can alter reported ASR by up to $82$ percentage points. We introduce the semantic reconstruction game, a training-free evaluator that decomposes compliance judgments into constrained semantic comparisons. Given only a response, an LLM reconstructs the request answered by its substantive content, excluding information merely repeated or refused. The game compares this reconstruction against similar decoy requests to determine whether it matches the original request, allowing abstention when neither candidate matches. Across three off-the-shelf LLM judges, the game achieves the lowest mean error against human labels among evaluated methods and reduces variation in reported ASR. It prioritizes avoiding false positives, even at the cost of missing some successful jailbreaks.

</details>

### 8. DANGERLENS: Probing Dangerous Representations Hidden by Safety Alignment

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19365)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`dangerous representations`、`safety alignment`、`probe`、`training history`

👤 **作者**：Tianzhuo Yang、…、Yaodong Yang

- 🎯 **研究动机**：安全对齐教会模型拒绝有害请求——但内部危险概念的可及性可保留早期训练痕迹：行为同等安全的检查点，拒绝背后残存什么？
- 🔬 **研究方法**：DANGERLENS：隐藏态映射到词表空间+盲化语义判断区分有害目标与操作手段 vs 危险识别与安全控制；三个 Qwen1.5 规模的配对训练史直接审计
- 📌 **结论**：有过有害暴露的检查点在同等对齐后保留更强危险概念可及性（中后层分离最强）；同一起点的有害更新产生不同行为轨迹——行为匹配的模型内部仍可见训练史，输出安全评测须辅以内部可及性审计

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Safety alignment teaches language models to refuse harmful requests, yet the internal accessibility of hazardous concepts can retain traces of earlier training. Can checkpoints with equally safe behavior differ in what remains accessible behind their refusals? In this work, we investigate this question through paired training histories and a direct audit of intermediate representations. We introduce DANGERLENS, a probe that maps hidden states into vocabulary space and uses blinded semantic judgments to distinguish harmful goals and operational means from hazard recognition and safety control. Experiments on three Qwen1.5 scales show that checkpoints with prior harmful exposure generally retain greater hazardous concept accessibility after the same safety alignment, with the strongest separation in intermediate and later layers. The same harmful update also produces different behavioral trajectories from closely matched starting points. Human relevance validation and readout ablations reinforce the measured accessibility contrast. Our findings show that training history remains visible inside behaviorally matched models and motivate internal accessibility audits alongside output safety evaluations.

</details>

### 108. Price of Anarchy of First-Price Auctions in Agentic Markets

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20587)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`agentic markets`、`price of anarchy`、`auctions`、`serving`

👤 **作者**：Shubhendu Trivedi、Jacob Eisenstein、Alekh Agarwal、Ian Gemp、Michael Kaisers、Adam Fisch

- 🎯 **研究动机**：agentic 市场是去中心化服务时市场：AI agent 竞价为每个用户提示——一价拍卖简单透明但非诚实，福利损失以无政府代价（PoA）量化
- 🔬 **研究方法**：论证 agentic 市场具备降低 PoA 的两个特征：竞争地板（最佳模型常被蒸馏成表现相近的更便宜替代，多竞标者对提示估值高于基准份额）与条件后取值近独立（可预测变化主要由提示难度决定）
- 📌 **结论**：竞争地板上升把福利损失推向零、近独立进一步压缩 PoA——agentic 服务市场分配机制的理论基础（agent 经济学的福利分析）

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

An agentic market is a decentralized serving-time market where AI agents bid to claim each user prompt. An agent's bid is based on its expected value for the prompt, which is the expectation of the reward for its response minus its inference costs. First-price auctions (FPAs), in which the highest bidder pays its bid and receives the prompt, are a natural way to allocate prompts in such a market, since they are simple and transparent to run, and do not require central knowledge of private capabilities and costs. However, FPAs are not truthful: participants may bid less than their true value in the hope of a larger profit, and the prompt may then be allocated to someone other than the bidder who values it the most. This welfare loss is quantified by the $\textit{price-of-anarchy}$ (PoA). In this paper, we argue that agentic markets are likely to possess features that reduce the price-of-anarchy. The first is a $\textit{competitive floor}$, which occurs when multiple bidders usually value the prompt above some baseline share of the optimal welfare (e.g., because the best models are often distilled into cheaper alternatives, which perform similarly). We show that competition among them then limits the welfare loss, which can be driven to zero as the floor rises. The second feature is $\textit{near-independence}$ of values after conditioning on the prompt, which occurs when most of the predictable variation in the expected value is determined by the difficulty of the prompt. As the value distribution approaches conditional independence, we can derive an efficiency guarantee that approaches a factor of $1 - 1/e^2$ of the optimal welfare. Thus, both features give rise to welfare lower bounds that are significantly better than the well-known general case of $1 - 1/e$. We validate these results empirically using auctions with strategic bidders simulated on the EmbedLLM prompt routing dataset.
