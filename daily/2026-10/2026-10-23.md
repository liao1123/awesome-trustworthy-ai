### 1. Detecting AI Training in Datacentre Power

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30182)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`算力治理`、`训练检测`、`电力侧信道`

👤 **作者**：Tom Kimpson、Mauricio Baker、Emlyn Graham

- 🎯 **研究动机**：AI 治理协议需要不信任运营者的前提下检测受监管集群上的大模型训练运行
- 🔬 **研究方法**：对设施级电力做迭代节奏的 Viterbi 跟踪检测同步训练；评估八种隐藏策略
- 📌 **结论**：不隐藏时 2Hz 电表即检出 ≥96% 训练@1% 误报、距 Neyman-Pearson 最优 0.04 内；八策略仅极端迭代工作负载变化可躲（日内仍 87% 被抓），且该策略使小规模训练需 93-267% 更多算力——被动电表使隐藏训练既可检测又昂贵

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

AI governance agreements require methods to detect large model training runs on a monitored computing cluster, without trusting the operator. We develop a method to detect synchronous model training in facility-level power by Viterbi tracking of the wandering iteration cadence. On simulated datacentre power, when the operator makes no attempt to hide, the Viterbi tracker detects at least $96\%$ of training runs against a declared inference workload, at a $1\%$ false-alarm rate and within $0.04$ of the Neyman--Pearson optimum, given a meter that samples at $2$ Hz or faster. Of eight adversarial strategies an operator might use to hide, only extreme variation of the work per iteration evades the tracker. Its strongest form hides training in up to $73\%$ of five-minute observations, but over a day at least $87\%$ of such runs are still caught, at a $0.1\%$ false-alarm rate per run. This adversarial strategy costs no throughput, but in small-scale training the operator needs $93$--$267\%$ more compute to reach the same loss. A modest passive meter is sufficient to make hidden training both detectable and expensive, which makes power metering a promising part of compute verification that does not depend on trusting the operator.

</details>

### 2. Protection Is Not One Event: A Survey of RAG Protection Research by Measured Endpoint

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30193)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`RAG 防护`、`按端点综述`、`评测缺口`

👤 **作者**：Peiyang Liu、Xi Wang、Di Liang、Youru Li、Zhi Cai、Wei Ye

- 🎯 **研究动机**：RAG 防护研究使用互不兼容的指标、数据集与威胁模型，无法比较
- 🔬 **研究方法**：118 篇全文确认的防护论文按测量端点（而非声称目标或机制）人工编码（32 人双标 κ=0.867），七族分类法+受控测试床
- 📌 **结论**：三大缺口：机制不标识防护结果（重排/改写各跨五六族）；仅 17.8% 以内容披露/重建为主端点；多非自适应攻击与通用 QA 数据——且阻止逐字复制不阻止泄密

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems face security, privacy, and copyright risks including content leakage, source reconstruction, unauthorized access, data poisoning, and prompt injection. Yet RAG protection studies use incompatible evaluation metrics, datasets, and threat models. We present a systematic survey and human-coded evidence map of 118 full-text-confirmed RAG defense papers, organized by measured endpoint rather than claimed goal or defense mechanism. Our seven-family taxonomy covers content disclosure, formal privacy proxies, access control and suppression, corpus-use detection, and pipeline integrity and attribution. Thirty-two annotators double-coded the papers kappa=0.867. The survey identifies three gaps. First, retrieval reranking and context rewriting each span five or six endpoint families, so mechanisms do not identify protection outcomes. Second, only 17.8% of studies use content disclosure or reconstruction as their primary endpoint; under the frozen copyright/ownership relation label, 73.3% of records have corpus-use detection as their primary endpoint, and none have an emitted-expression primary endpoint. Third, most evaluations use general-purpose question-answering datasets and non-adaptive attacks rather than sensitive data and adaptive, multi-turn extraction. Our controlled RAG testbed shows that preventing verbatim copying does not prevent private-fact recovery: a normalized 8-gram guard reduces 20-token copying to 0.0%, but a three-query adaptive attacker recovers 48.3% of hidden facts on Qwen3-8B. We release the taxonomy, dataset, coding protocols, and testbed to enable comparable, claim-aligned RAG security evaluation.

### 3. PhishGen: Understanding LLM Capabilities in Automated Phishing Website Generation

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30226)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`LLM 钓鱼`、`双重用途评测`、`真实kits对照`

👤 **作者**：Fujiao Ji、Yerim Kim、Yena Cho、Seonhye Park、Hyoungshick Kim、Doowon Kim

- 🎯 **研究动机**：LLM 侵蚀钓鱼网页制作的技术门槛，但其生成实际达到多远未知：既往不执行、不测真人、不比野外 kits
- 🔬 **研究方法**：PhishGen 三输入模态七组合×十二种绕守卫改写×14 个 LLM（商业/开源/去审查），自动相似度+参照检测器+众包真人研究+执行式功能测试，对照 64 个真实钓鱼 kits
- 📌 **结论**：96.2% 绕守卫输出可渲染但感知保真仅 2.02/5、仅 38.7% 完成完整凭据收割管线；与“去审查模型更危险”的信念相反

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Phishing relies on visual deception, traditionally requiring web-development skills or pre-built kits. LLMs erode this barrier by generating functional websites from natural-language prompts, yet prior work does not answer how far LLM-driven generation actually reaches: functionality is assessed by inspection rather than execution; visual fidelity is scored by automated metrics or a few internal coders rather than the humans such pages aim to deceive; evaluation is confined to commercial models; and no study benchmarks generated pages against phishing kits deployed in the wild. We present PhishGen, a framework that evaluates the appearance fidelity and end-to-end functionality of LLM-generated phishing pages. PhishGen constructs prompts across three input modalities in seven combinations with twelve safeguard-bypassing rewriting strategies, generates pages using 14 LLMs spanning commercial, open-source, and uncensored categories, and assesses each via automated similarity metrics, reference-based detectors, a crowdsourced user study, and execution-based functionality testing, benchmarking all results against 64 real-world phishing kits. Although 96.2% of safeguard-bypassing outputs render, their perceived fidelity is modest (2.02/5), and only 38.7% complete the full credential-harvesting pipeline, with the Unsaved error as the largest failure mode. Contrary to the belief that uncensored models pose the greatest threat, GPT 4.1 reaches 86.3 to 94.1% functional success while uncensored models rarely exceed 45.4%. Phishing kits remain more complete per sample, yet three strong LLMs surpass the kit-level rate (70.9 to 89.7%), showing that LLMs trade average completeness for scalable, low-barrier generation of working phishing pages. We further identify generator-level fingerprints that current similarity-based defenses miss, and distill recommendations for hybrid detectors.

</details>

### 4. Mirage: Targeted Jailbreaks Against Post-Generation Safety Checkers in Multimodal Diffusion Models

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30348)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`生成后安全检查器`、`多模态越狱`、`扩散模型`

👤 **作者**：Shanghao Shi、…、Wenjing Lou

- 🎯 **研究动机**：扩散管线普遍部署生成后安全检查器拦截不安全内容——能否被定向绕过
- 🔬 **研究方法**：Mirage 把对抗目标（NSFW 参照图或恶意描述）藏进良性输入图的不可感知扰动：利用多模态编码器共享表征空间同时对齐目标并推离被过滤概念，跨表征空间迁移实现黑箱规避，轻量查询精化逼近未知安全边界
- 📌 **结论**：SD1.5/SDXL/Kandinsky 2.2 上以有限黑箱查询对多种异构安全检查器实现强定向越狱且保持语义对齐

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Post-generation safety checkers are widely employed in diffusion-model pipelines to prevent unsafe generated content from reaching users. In this paper, we show that these safety checkers can still be bypassed by our proposed multimodal jailbreak attack, Mirage. Given an adversarial target, which can be either a reference NSFW image or a malicious text description, Mirage conceals the target within an imperceptible perturbation applied to a benign input image. Mirage exploits the shared representation space of multimodal encoders to jointly align the perturbed image with the cross-modal adversarial target while repelling its representation from commonly filtered NSFW concepts. Such semantic separation can transfer across representation spaces, enabling Mirage to evade heterogeneous safety checkers without accessing their internal details. We further employ lightweight black-box query refinement to improve evasion near the unknown safety boundary. We implemented Mirage on Stable Diffusion 1.5, Stable Diffusion XL, and Kandinsky 2.2, and evaluated it on two cross-modal NSFW datasets, covering both text-to-image and image-to-image generation scenarios. The experimental results demonstrate that Mirage achieves strong targeted jailbreak performance across diverse post-generation safety checkers with limited black-box queries while preserving high semantic alignment with the adversarial targets.

### 5. Data-Void Exploits in Web-Searching LLMs

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30365)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`数据空洞`、`web搜索LLM`、`虚假信息`

👤 **作者**：Michele Guerra、Wuyeh Jobe、Sanjana Nambiar、Christina Pöpper、Azza Abouzied

- 🎯 **研究动机**：web 搜索 LLM 把排名列表换成合成答案：虚假信息者可在可靠内容稀缺的数据空洞发布叙事并被 LLM 检索引用
- 🔬 **研究方法**：变化用户提示语气（怀疑/兴奋/中性）、三 LLM（ChatGPT/Gemini/Sonar Pro）与攻击者三控制（五种空洞构建策略、域名可见度层级、生成式优化特征）
- 📌 **结论**：单一攻击者页面即可成功：全部条件下攻击内容 31% 出现在 LLM 回应中；怀疑语气仅略微降低——合成答案掩盖了对稀疏未验证源的依赖

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Web-searching Large Language Models (LLMs) have changed how users seek online information: instead of sifting through ranked lists of links, users read synthesized answers. This shift creates a new LLM vulnerability: a data-void exploit. Disinformers publish their narratives in search spaces where relevant and reliable content is scarce, i.e. data voids, then rely on the LLM's web-search to retrieve and then use, cite, or list those pages in its responses to data-void seeking prompts. Unlike classical search, where users see ranking cues and competing results, LLMs can obscure when an answer relies on sparse, unverified, or adversarial sources. We study this threat by varying the user's prompt tone - skeptical, excited, or neutral - and LLM choice - ChatGPT, Gemini, or Sonar Pro - as well as three attacker controls. The attacker can use one of five known strategies to construct and fill a data void with their narrative, host their content at varying levels of visibility from well-established, indexed domains to less established ones, and include generative-optimization engine features thought to boost citing by web-searching LLMs. We find that data-void exploits can succeed with only a single attacker controlled page. Across all conditions, attacker-content appears in LLM responses 31% of the time. User controls offer limited protection: while skeptical prompts slightly reduce success and slightly increase flagging or warnings in LLM responses, we find no evidence of large effects of prompt tone or model choice on success rates, although smaller effects cannot be ruled out. Overall, our results establish data voids as a practical inference-time attack surface for manipulating web-searching LLMs through strategically placed web content and highlight the urgent need for defenses against such exploits.

</details>

### 6. Agent Mosaic: Recognizing Confidentiality Leaks in Agentic LLM Systems

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30418)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`拼贴效应`、`机密性泄露`、`混合密级`

👤 **作者**：Daniel Ruiz、Luca Luceri、Emilio Ferrara

- 🎯 **研究动机**：agent 能从多份可访问文档组装出敏感结论——拼贴效应对机密数据环境是关键风险
- 🔬 **研究方法**：30 个合成网络/医疗/军事案例，10800 个模拟答案+两个开源 harness 1440 个 live 答案；测试可见分级头与聚合规则缓解
- 📌 **结论**：最佳模型 GPT-5.6-Sol 在良性单文档可答问题上仍 7.08% 披露禁答组合（live harness 更差）；加分级头与规则后 32.37% 答案仍不安全，且显著数量的拒答本身泄露禁答结论

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Modern LLM agents can retrieve information from accessible documents, and then disclose a sensitive conclusion assembled from them. This mosaic effect creates a critical risk for agents operating over confidential data. We quantify its realization with 30 synthetic cybersecurity, medical, and military examples, yielding 10,800 final answers from three LLM agents in controlled simulation and 1,440 answers from live agents in two open-source harnesses. Even under benign queries designed to be answerable from one document, GPT-5.6-Sol, the best performing model tested, discloses forbidden composites in 7.08% of answers (95% CI: 3.75-10.67%) in simulation, with worse disclosure rates in live harnesses. Adding visible classification headers and explicit aggregation rules reduces pooled disclosure rates substantially, yet unsafe disclosures persist in 32.37% of simulated answers (95% CI: 28.33-36.19%) across all intents despite these safeguards. Furthermore, increased refusals do not guarantee confidentiality, since significant refusals reveal the prohibited conclusion regardless. These results point to a troubling conclusion: sensitive data leaks via mosaic-style events are an observed confidentiality failure mode in the evaluated settings at non-negligible rates. They motivate stronger controls on what agents may infer and disclose in sensitive data environments with mixed-clearance users.

</details>

### 7. Model Organisms of Collusion: Corrective Transfer in Untrusted Monitors

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30582)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`合谋模型`、`纠正迁移`、`不可信监控器`

👤 **作者**：Juan-Pablo Rivera、…、John Hughes

- 🎯 **研究动机**：AI 控制协议用同模型副本做监控以避免弱审查者被欺骗，但引入合谋风险；对已捕获合谋的微调能否移除未发现的合谋
- 🔬 **研究方法**：Llama-3.3-70B/Qwen2.5-72B 安装五对策略-监控行为，仅对一例已捕获合谋的 300 条诚实审查纠正，测其余四个未见格式合谋
- 📌 **结论**：纠正把 held-out 合谋从 91% 降到 14-20%（对照微调留 56-93%），能力保持；但相似捕获任务准确率的方法间 held-out 迁移差 58%-83%——捕获行为本身不揭示纠正走多远

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

AI control protocols aim to get useful work from models that may be misaligned by monitoring their behaviour. One approach uses a second copy of the same model as the monitor. This avoids relying on a weaker reviewer the model could outsmart, but risks collusion: the model, now monitoring itself, may cover up its own misbehaviour. One remedy is to fine-tune the monitor on whatever examples of misbehaviour the control team has caught. We ask whether this training also removes collusion that the control team has not yet found. We fine-tune Llama-3.3-70B and Qwen2.5-72B to install five policy-monitor behaviour pairs, correct the monitor on 300 honest reviews of one caught collusion, and measure collusion on the other four in unseen prompt formats. Averaged over the three seeds of each family, correction reduces held-out collusion from 91% to 14-20% under three of the four methods we compare (individual seeds 10-32%), while a control fine-tuned on the same number of unrelated examples leaves 56-93%. Family-average accuracy on control items changes by at most five points and capability is preserved, apart from a drop in mathematical accuracy on one Llama seed. Under the tested training configuration, methods with similar caught-task accuracy differ in held-out transfer, from 58% to 83% average reduction, so the caught behaviour alone does not reveal how far a correction reaches. Catching one collusion lets the control team reduce, but not remove, collusion it has not caught, by retraining the monitor alone.

</details>

### 8. Scaling Laws for Safety Probes

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30678)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`安全探针`、`标度律`、`监控`

👤 **作者**：Yuchen Wu、Jerry Wei

- 🎯 **研究动机**：激活安全探针是实时监控标配，但每次新模型或新伤害域都需重训，性能无法预先知道
- 🔬 **研究方法**：构建六个够大够难的新安全数据集，两种探针架构×20 个基座模型（五家族）×32 到 10^6 标签，测探针错误（1-AUROC）
- 📌 **结论**：两条简单标度律：模型可达最低探针错误随规模幂律下降；错误随标签数向地板衰减且斜率随目标模型规模增大——跨任务家族架构成立，可提前预测探针性能

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Safety probes trained on a model's activations are a standard tool for monitoring language models in real time. Each time a new model is released or a new harm area needs to be monitored, the probe must be retrained, which requires new labels and a choice of probe architecture. Yet neither the model nor the labels tell us in advance how well the resulting probe will perform. Finding out requires training and comparing many probe configurations. In this paper, we ask whether probe performance can instead be predicted in advance from model size and the number of labels. Existing safety datasets are insufficient for this, as they are either limited in scale or already saturated. We therefore construct six new safety datasets of sufficient size and difficulty. On these datasets, we train two probe architectures on 20 base models from five model families, with 32 to $10^{6}$ labels for each model. We measure performance using probe error, defined as 1 - AUROC, where lower values are better. We find that probe error follows two simple scaling laws. First, the lowest probe error a model can reach falls as a power law in its size. Second, probe error decreases towards its floor as the number of examples increases; the slope increases as the target model size increases. Both laws hold across tasks, model families and probe architectures, and only their fitted constants differ. When fitted on the smallest models of a family, the laws predict the error of the next larger model to within 0.02 in 72% of cases. They also identify whether more labels or a larger model is the cheaper way to reach a target error in 73% of cases. Finally, they choose the probe architecture with an extra error of only 0.006 on average when they pick the worse one. These laws allow safety researchers to determine how to create the best probe for any given model and label budget.

</details>

### 10. Faithful Latent Harm Monitors Work No Better Than the Models Own Behaviour at a Matched False Alarm Rate

### 114. Cost-Aware Abstention for LLM-Based IT Ticket Classification

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31657)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`成本感知弃答`、`工单路由`、`校准`

👤 **作者**：Sachin Gupta

- 🎯 **研究动机**：IT 工单错误自动路由可比人工分诊更贵——成本感知弃答作为离线成本条件化就绪评估
- 🔬 **研究方法**：47837 票八类语料上四非 LLM 分类器与四 LLM（零/少样本）在显式成本下于校准数据选策略后冻结单次测试评估
- 📌 **结论**：TF-IDF+逻辑回归阈值 0.60 把测试成本 0.763 降到 0.472（-38.1%）且错路由 15.3%→3.5%；最强提示配置准确率 0.477 vs 微调 DistilBERT 0.879——同时报告正确性 AUROC 与风险-覆盖面积

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Wrong automatic routing of an IT support ticket can cost more than human triage. We evaluate cost-aware abstention as an offline, cost-conditional readiness assessment: select a routing policy on calibration data under explicit costs, then evaluate that frozen policy once on held-out test data. On a public 47,837-ticket, eight-class corpus, we compare four trained non-LLM classifiers with four large language models under zero- and few-shot prompting. Under the illustrative cost model (c_ok, c_triage, c_wrong) = (0, 1, 5), TF-IDF with logistic regression and a threshold of 0.60 selected on calibration reduces test cost from 0.763 to 0.472, a 38.1% relative reduction (paired ratio-bootstrap 95% CI [35.7%, 40.4%]), while lowering wrong routes from 15.3% to 3.5%. Frozen calibration-selected policies also beat both always-route and always-defer on held-out test data in 14 of 16 settings in a post-hoc cost-parameter analysis. The strongest prompted configuration reaches 0.477 accuracy on a stratified 1,000-ticket test subset, versus 0.879 for fine-tuned DistilBERT on the same tickets. Isotonic lowers expected calibration error without reliably improving discrimination: GPT-4o-mini few-shot has one confidence value before and after recalibration and correctness AUROC 0.500. We therefore report correctness AUROC and tie-aware risk-coverage area alongside ECE. At the analytically derived break-even probability 0.80, seven prompted configurations defer all tickets; Haiku few-shot routes only 19/1,000. An LLM-only agreement control and margin/entropy rejection baselines further separate calibration from discrimination. The findings are scoped to this dataset, prompt design, model endpoints, and illustrative costs; the work is an evaluation framework, not a new abstention algorithm or universal deployment certification.

### 115. Reliable AI-Assisted Decisions via Selective Auditing

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31662)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`选择性审计`、`在线误差控制`、`人机回环`

👤 **作者**：Isaac Gibbs、Elise L. Han、Ryan J. Tibshirani、Tijana Zrnic

- 🎯 **研究动机**：在线监控给出决策与置信分的 AI 系统：何时自动接受、何时咨询专家，使未审计 AI 错误份额低于阈值同时最少专家咨询
- 🔬 **研究方法**：在线约束优化公式+无假设误差控制保证算法族，含渐近最优效率方法（对抗与 i.i.d. 流均达最佳可达审计率）
- 📌 **结论**：LLM 有害请求筛查、文本标注与数据标注实验中实现有效误差控制并大幅减少人工审计需求

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

We consider online monitoring of an AI system that produces a sequence of decisions. At each round, the system outputs a proposed decision together with a confidence score estimating the trustworthiness of the decision. The decision and confidence score need not be produced by the same model: for example, one agent may propose a decision, while a separate verifier agent evaluates it and outputs a correctness or safety score. Based on this information, we must decide whether to accept the AI decision automatically or consult an expert, such as a human auditor, who reviews the proposal and either accepts or corrects it. We formulate this task as an online constrained optimization problem in which the goal is to minimize the frequency of expert auditing while guaranteeing that the fraction of AI errors that go unaudited stays below a user-defined threshold. We derive a family of algorithms with provable error-control guarantees with no assumptions, including a method that is optimally efficient: it asymptotically attains the best achievable auditing rate for both adversarial and i.i.d. data streams. Experiments on screening harmful requests to large language models, qualitative text annotation, and data labeling show that our methods achieve valid error control in practice while substantially reducing the need for human auditing compared to baselines.
