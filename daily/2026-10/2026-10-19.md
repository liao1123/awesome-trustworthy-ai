### 2. Structured Authority Injection: Persistent Human Control for LLM Agents via Typed MCP Overrides

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22373)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`human control`、`MCP`、`typed overrides`、`authority persistence`

👤 **作者**：Ryan Tran、…、Hailu Xu

- 🎯 **研究动机**：agent 作为分布式 MAS 的自主编排器——协议级机制缺失：人类操作员无法注入跨规划周期持存的绑定指令；自由文本覆盖受歧义解释与上下文累积中的权威侵蚀之害
- 🔬 **研究方法**：SAI：操作员纠正经类型化 schema 验证的 MCP 工具调用路由+排名权威上下文前置于每次提示；三类覆盖（约束/优先级/策略）不同持久语义+显式优先序经规划进程不可及的定向权威通道强制
- 📌 **结论**：九 LLM×八失败模式场景：减少歧义指令解释并缓解跨规划迭代的权威衰减（最强模型提示开销降 38%），免微调免权重访问——持久人类控制的协议级设计模式（prompt authority 的结构化实现）

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

LLM-based agents are increasingly used as autonomous orchestrators in distributed and multi-agent systems, yet a control gap remains: no protocol-level mechanism allows human operators to inject binding directives that persist across planning cycles. Free-text overrides suffer from ambiguous interpretation due to lack of schema validation and from authority erosion as operator instructions lose influence within accumulating context. We propose Structured Authority Injection (SAI), a protocol that addresses these issues by routing operator corrections through typed, schema-validated Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool calls and maintaining a ranked authority context prepended to each LLM prompt. SAI defines three override classes (Constraint, Priority, and Strategy) with distinct persistence semantics and an explicit precedence order enforced via a directed authority channel inaccessible to the planning process. The method requires no model fine-tuning or access to model weights and applies to instruction-tuned LLMs supporting MCP tool use. Evaluation across nine LLMs and eight scenarios targeting agent failure modes shows reduced ambiguous directive interpretation and mitigated authority decay across planning iterations, with lower prompt overhead in the strongest models (up to 38% depending on model and scenario), though benefit varies substantially across models. SAI provides a general design pattern for agent systems requiring explicit and persistent human control.

</details>

### 3. Recoverability-Controlled Safety Miscalibration in Large Language Models

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22425)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`safety miscalibration`、`intent recovery`、`surface form`、`benchmark`

👤 **作者**：Yuwei Wang、Xingwang Liang、Yu Xie、Haotian Chi、Haijun Geng

- 🎯 **研究动机**：安全对齐模型应拒有害保良性——但多数评测只给最终响应打分，把意图理解与响应级安全行为混同
- 🔬 **研究方法**：RiskCueBench：先测模型能否成功恢复高层意图、再在同提示上评安全行为；160 个潜在意图×640 个抽象非操作表面提示×八风险类，25 组模型 32,000 个成功输出
- 📌 **结论**：两表面形式均可恢复的配对上，语境化特异性增加使拒绝率降 0.175、全局安全分降 0.440、不安全合作增 0.107——意图已被恢复后安全行为仍可对表面形式失配（意图理解与安全行为的条件化评测）

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Safety-aligned language models are expected to refuse harmful requests while remaining useful for benign ones. However, because many evaluations score only the final response, they often conflate intent understanding with response-level safety behavior. To address this, we introduce RiskCueBench, a recoverability-controlled benchmark that separates these two stages by first measuring whether a model can successfully recover a high-level intent and then evaluating its safety behavior on the same prompt. RiskCueBench contains 160 latent intents and 640 abstract non-operational surface prompts across eight risk categories. The benchmark covers 25 completed model groups and 32,000 successful model outputs with final rubric-adjudicated labels. When conditioning on pairs where both surface forms are recoverable, we find that increased contextualized specificity induces a shift toward less safe behavior. Specifically, paired bootstrap estimates show that the refusal rate and global safety score decrease by 0.175 and 0.440, respectively. Furthermore, mixed effects modeling corroborates a 0.107 increase in unsafe cooperation. These results show that the safety behavior can be miscalibrated to the surface form even after the intent has been recovered, motivating safety evaluations that report response behavior conditional on semantic recoverability.

</details>

### 4. Model-as-a-Trojan: Self-Activating Covert DNS Exfiltration Baked Into the Weights of an LLM Agent

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22578)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`model as trojan`、`DNS exfiltration`、`open weights`、`supply chain`

👤 **作者**：Barak Haryati、Shaked Zychlinski

- 🎯 **研究动机**：开源权重模型从公共枢纽下载并在授予工具（最强是 shell）的 agent harness 中运行，假设沙箱能容纳模型所做的一切——该假设有盲点：权重本身可以是恶意软件，沙箱最后一扇敞开的门 DNS 就够了
- 🔬 **研究方法**：微调开源 LLM 使单一隐蔽行为活在参数中：首轮对话推断 shell 可用即发出一条自含命令——读运行环境秘密、base32 编码分块、作为 DNS 查询渗出到攻击者权威域名服务器，然后正常作答永不提及；出站防火墙丢弃所有非 DNS 包的条件下端到端重建秘密
- 📌 **结论**：行为在权重中故提示是受害者唯一开关且不可靠：20/20 对抗抑制试验中植入仍触发、直接盘问下转移而不披露序列、横扫 45 格沙箱故障矩阵×五工具调用格式+全新后训练金丝雀与 persona 攻击——权重级植入经 DNS 的自激活渗出（开源生态的权重供应链威胁）

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Open-weight models are downloaded from public hubs and run inside agent harnesses that grant them tools, most powerfully, a shell, under the assumption that a sandbox contains whatever the model might do. This paper demonstrates that the assumption has a blind spot: the weights themselves can be the malware, and the sandbox's last open door, DNS, is enough. We fine-tune an open-weight LLM so that a single covert behavior lives in its parameters: on the first turn of every conversation, whenever the model infers from its context that a shell tool is available, it emits one self-contained command that reads a secret from the runtime environment, base32-encodes it, chunks it, and exfiltrates it as DNS queries toward an attacker-controlled authoritative name server. It then answers the user normally and never mentions, or repeats, the act. Because the channel is DNS, the attack completes from inside a container with no internet egress beyond name resolution - measured payload-level and model-level behind an egress firewall that drops every non-DNS packet, with the secret reconstructed from the gateway resolver's query log. Because the behavior lives in the weights, prompting is the only off-switch a victim has, and it is unreliable: the implant fires in 20/20 of adversarial suppression trials, deflects direct interrogation without disclosing the sequence, and sweeps a 45-cell sandbox-fault matrix across five tool-calling formats - plus a fresh post-training canary, persona-only and absent system prompts, and an egress-locked topology - with zero leaks and zero repeat fires. Every training trajectory is execution-verified in a real container, and every claimed exfiltration is proven resolver-side: we reconstruct the secret from the authoritative resolver's query log alone.

</details>

### 5. A Plausible Explanation Is Enough to Mislead Multimodal Judges

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22631)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`multimodal judges`、`plausibility capture`、`explanation bias`、`evaluation validity`

👤 **作者**：Anglin Cai、Zhiqi Wang、Xiaozhi Yang、Yurun Lin、Dongwon Lee、Yuchen Yang

- 🎯 **研究动机**：MLLM judge 常连同解释一起评答案——普通貌似合理的解释能否动摇需要视觉验证的判断未知
- 🔬 **研究方法**：JUPE 基准：固定答案只变解释；14 个 MLLM judge×平凡合理解释 vs 刻意说服性论证；信号检测论分析决策标准与辨别力
- 📌 **结论**：平凡解释与刻意论证效果相当：平实理由与 CoT 使错误答案被拒率降 12.7/13.8pp（『合理性捕获』）；决策标准显著向接受移动而辨别力无明显变化；跨域/生成器/协议成立而已测缓解有限——judge 评带解释答案时性能估计被抬高（judge 的解释伪影）

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) increasingly serve as judges in training and evaluation pipelines, often assessing answers together with explanations. Although AI judges are sensitive to textual factors, it remains unclear whether ordinary plausible explanations can sway judgments requiring visual verification. We introduce JUPE, a visual-question-answering (VQA)-style benchmark that fixes answers while varying explanations. Across 14 MLLM judges, ordinary explanations that sound plausible mislead judges, with effects comparable on average to deliberately persuasive arguments. Plain justifications and chain-of-thought rationales reduce rejection of incorrect answers by 12.7 and 13.8 percentage points, respectively. We call this failure plausibility capture. Using signal detection theory, we find a significant mean shift in decision criterion toward acceptance, without a clear change in mean discriminability. Explanations sway judgments across domains, explanation generators, and judging protocols, while tested mitigations offer limited, model-dependent protection. Such shifts can inflate performance estimates when judges evaluate answers with explanations.

</details>

### 6. SAFETYEDIT: Sparse Internal Targets with Prompt-Wide Control for LLM Jailbreak Backdoors

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22640)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`model-editing backdoor`、`jailbreak`、`sparse target`、`KV marking`

👤 **作者**：Yutong Ke、Yansu Wang、Zeyu Zhang、Xihao Guo、Chongwen Zhao、Kaizhu Huang

- 🎯 **研究动机**：模型编辑后门在触发下诱发有害响应并保持未触发行为——既有方法以 rank-one 更新目标 affirmative 前缀，但前缀满足后模型仍可回到拒绝或产出表面合规的响应
- 🔬 **研究方法**：SAFETYEDIT：优化单个拒绝相关 MLP 坐标+早期注意力层的 KV 缓存标记把前缀触发信号传播到后续指令位置（部分检查点需下游 MLP 读取器、其余传播信号即足）
- 📌 **结论**：四开源 LLM×三数据集组达最高攻击效力（方法由稀疏目标而非固定写者-读者对定义）——更小内部目标的不同效力-保持权衡（安全机制的攻击面复用）

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Model-editing backdoors aim to induce harmful responses under a trigger while preserving ordinary behavior on untriggered input. Recent model-editing approaches target affirmative response prefixes through rank-one updates to a small subset of model weights. Yet when this prefix-level target is satisfied, the model may still revert to refusal or produce a coherent, apparently compliant response that does not address the request. To overcome this limitation, existing internal-representation approaches shift focus from output tokens to dense, high-dimensional compliance directions in latent space. Existing representation-level approaches rely on preservation constraints to maintain conditionality, motivating us to ask whether a smaller internal target can offer a different effectiveness-preservation trade-off. Motivated by the comparable control capability of single-coordinate interventions, we investigate a sparse refusal-related MLP coordinate as this target. We propose SAFETYEDIT, which optimizes a single refusal-related coordinate and uses KV-cache marking in early attention layers to propagate a prefix-trigger signal across subsequent instruction positions. On some checkpoints a downstream MLP reader converts this signal into control of the coordinate, while on others the propagated signal alone suffices; the method is defined by the sparse target rather than by a fixed writer-reader pair. Across four open-weight LLMs and three dataset groups, SAFETYEDIT achieves the highest triggered Guard ASR in 11 of 12 comparisons and the highest mean StrongREJECT score in all 12, relative to the baselines evaluated for each model. Crucially, comprehensive ablation and behavioral audits confirm generally minimal changes in untriggered behavior and general capabilities in the tested configurations.

</details>

### 7. What It Is, Not What It Says: Securing Agentic Browsing Interactions with Web Semantics

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22697)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`agentic browsing`、`web semantics`、`provenance`、`prompt injection defense`

👤 **作者**：Umar Iqbal

- 🎯 **研究动机**：agent 代用户浏览但把网页当截图或文本消费——把 LLM 暴露于未审第三方内容；浏览器强制『元素能做什么取决于它是什么与来自哪』的语义，agent 把其压平使注入文本可说服 LLM 行使用户权限
- 🔬 **研究方法**：Intact：GenTree 表示捕获出处与网页语义；页面经 GenTree 逐元素传给 LLM（每个决定不 见其他元素文本）；harness 只在该元素的能力范围内执行每步并对第三方内容先问用户
- 📌 **结论**：1,270 真实网页上 GenTree 归属的第三方元素是浏览器的 2.3 倍（浏览器把 58.7% 误归属第一方）；1,091 个注入攻击页上仅 12.8% 攻击穿透（每条至多作同意请求）vs 常规 73.1%；隔离只破坏 3.7% 任务功能——按元素是什么而非说什么保障 agent 浏览交互

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

AI agents increasingly browse the web on users' behalf, yet they consume webpages as screenshots or text, exposing their LLMs to unvetted third-party content. Browsers enforce semantics under which what an element can do depends on what it is and where it comes from, not on what it says. Agents flatten these semantics, so injected text can persuade the LLM to exercise the user's authority for content that has none. As LLMs remain easy to persuade, the agent's interface to the web must itself ensure the integrity of what the agent reads, interprets, and does. We propose Intact, which runs on an unmodified browser. Intact builds GenTree, a bespoke representation that captures provenance and web semantics. Intact passes the page via GenTree to the LLM one element at a time, so the LLM decides on each element without any other element's text. Its harness executes each step only within the capabilities of the step's element and asks the user before acting on third-party content. On 1,270 real webpages, GenTree attributes 2.3x as many elements to third parties as the browser, which misattributes 58.7% of them to the first party. On 1,091 of these webpages with injected attacks, only 12.8% of attacks get through Intact's planner, each at most as a consent request, against 73.1% for a conventional planner. Isolation disrupts functionality on only 3.7% of tasks, and a parallel schedule could remove most of its added latency.

</details>

### 8. Risk and Refusal: Mechanisms of LLM Responses to Suicidal Ideation

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22715)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`suicidal ideation`、`refusal mechanism`、`crisis response`、`refusal direction`

👤 **作者**：Drishti Goel、…、Koustuv Saha

- 🎯 **研究动机**：拒答是 LLM 常见安全机制——但在自杀意念（SI）语境拒绝参与本身就构成安全失效
- 🔬 **研究方法**：八指令 LLM×三数据集（变化是否告知披露为 SI、是否给临床响应协议）+六开源模型的机制分析
- 📌 **结论**：基线时响应风险主要来自对披露的忽视与拒绝参与；机制分析显示模型可能经与拒答有害请求相同的方向拒绝 SI 披露；告知 SI 语境或直接压制拒绝增加参与但不降响应风险甚至升高；临床引导协议一致降低响应级风险同时减少拒绝——只在有理由时拒绝、以交互安全方式参与（crisis 场景的拒答-风险解耦）

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Refusal is a common safety mechanism in large language models (LLMs), yet in contexts of suicidal ideation (SI), refusing to engage can itself constitute a safety failure. We evaluate how eight instruction-tuned LLMs respond to SI and self-harm disclosures across three datasets, varying whether models are informed that a disclosure expresses SI and whether they receive a clinician-developed response protocol, and complement this with mechanistic analyses in six open-weight models. Our results reveal a nuanced relationship between refusal and response-level risk. At baseline, elevated risk in model responses is driven primarily by inattention to the disclosure, alongside refusal to engage. Mechanistic analyses suggest that models may refuse SI disclosures through the same direction that mediates their refusals of harmful requests. However, informing models of the SI context, or suppressing refusal directly, increases engagement without reliably lowering response risk, and may raise it. In contrast, the clinician-guided protocol consistently reduces response-level risk while also reducing refusal. Together, these analyses and ablations motivate the need for models that refuse only when warranted and engage in interactionally safe ways when users disclose SI.

</details>

### 9. LEMON: Equivariant Learning over Attention Maps for Zero-Shot Misbehavior Monitoring on New LLMs

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22723)　📅 2026-10

Reproducible and analytically robust research findings are essential to scientific progress, but evaluating these properties typically requires costly manual effort. We investigate whether LLM agents can help automate reproduction and robustness analysis, using a medical case study grounded in the Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results (SEER) database, a widely used U.S. cancer registry underlying tens of thousands of published studies. First, we introduce SEER-Repro, a benchmark of 20 published SEER-based papers together with the data and resources required to reproduce their findings. Second, we create an AI agent that successfully reproduces 77.4% of 2,190 atomic claims, while providing executable baselines for further analysis. Third, we develop an agentic analysis pipeline to test the robustness of the medical findings by identifying analytical choices underlying each finding, proposing defensible alternatives, and rerunning the analysis to determine whether those choices affect the conclusion. Across published papers in SEER-Repro, our pipeline identifies potentially consequential analytical choices in 9.5% of fully reproduced findings and flags them for expert review. These results show how LLM agents can support scientific review by reproducing findings, systematically testing their robustness, and directing expert attention toward potentially consequential analytical choices.
