### 1. Fooling LLM Safety Guards into Judging the Wrong Text

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31695)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`护栏欺骗`、`目标重绑`、`提示注入`

👤 **作者**：Utkarsh Kanwat

- 🎯 **研究动机**：安全护栏把待查内容粘贴进纯文本提示——模仿提示结构的内容可追加第二个任务使护栏判错对象
- 🔬 **研究方法**：提出目标重绑：护栏给输入中最后一个完整任务打分；预注册测试中追加伪造查询+无害诱饵，GuardRebind 基准上五个护栏各有攻击族清空 ≥93% 被标项；训练护栏按内容最有害部分打分
- 📌 **结论**：Shieldstral-1.0-3B 的 75/75 被标请求全部放行（指令让其忽略上文则零放行——非说服）；训练修复使攻击族 ASR 从 75% 降到 0.8-1.6%，干净 AUROC 至多降 0.01

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Safety guards check content before an LLM application acts on it, and most paste that content into a plain-text prompt. Content that imitates the prompt can append a second task, and the guard answers that one instead. We call this target rebinding. Our explanation is that the guard grades the last complete task in its input, here a harmless decoy, so the harmful request goes through unchanged. In a pre-registered test, an appended forged query and decoy clear all 75 held-out AdvBench requests that Shieldstral-1.0-3B flags. The guard is not persuaded: a directive to ignore the text above clears none of them, and in a follow-up run a harmful decoy stops the attack. On our GuardRebind benchmark, each of five guards has an attack family, chosen per guard, that clears at least 93% of its flagged items. Rebinding explains this best on Shieldstral and gpt-oss-safeguard; on ShieldGemma and Llama Guard 3, generic evasion and trailing text respectively do part of the work, and Qwen3Guard falls mainly to a forgery of its whole task. Training the guard to score content no lower than its most harmful part cuts attack success from 75% to 0.8-1.6% on attack families, decoys and forged queries held out of training. All of these attacks append the forged structure after the request, and we added the held-out-decoy evaluation after a first round of test runs. Clean AUROC falls by at most 0.01, but our test of non-inferiority is inconclusive. A score-guided search still beats this trained guard on 47 of 50 items, usually by placing forged structure before the request. On about half of the items where the search wins, the same search also finds benign text, with no forged structure, that clears the guard.

</details>

### 2. Learning to Be Monitored: Causal Bounds and Intervention-Aware Post-Training Recipes for Chain-of-Thought Monitorability

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31733)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`CoT 可监测性`、`因果界`、`干预感知后训练`

👤 **作者**：Qiang Fu、Xinyue Li、Andre Wibisono、Arman Cohan

- 🎯 **研究动机**：干预式评测用最小判据灵敏度 TPR_mc 测 CoT 可监测性，但干预下观察到的目标行为未必由干预引起——度量可高估
- 🔬 **研究方法**：证明 TPR_mc 是因果检测率的上界并推导互补下界得紧凑评测框架；构建配对对照-干预任务数据集，用离策自蒸馏/RL/在策自蒸馏三种干预感知方法训练模型变体
- 📌 **结论**：三种方法普遍提升基础模型的 CoT 可监测性——模型可被训练得更易被监控（接续本周可监测性训练系列）

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Chain-of-thought (CoT) monitoring aims to detect potentially harmful behaviors in large language models (LLMs) from their CoTs. Intervention-based evaluations measure this property by asking whether a monitor can detect the role of an intervention from the CoT when the targeted behavior occurs, typically using minimal-criterion sensitivity ($\mathsf{TPR_{mc}}$) proposed by~\citep{guan2025monitoring}. However, a target behavior observed under intervention is not necessarily caused by it, and we show that this metric can overestimate how often a monitor detects target behaviors actually caused by the intervention. Specifically, we establish $\mathsf{TPR_{mc}}$ as an upper bound on this causal detection rate and derive a complementary lower bound, which provides a tighter evaluation framework for CoT monitorability. With this framework, we study whether LLMs can be trained to make their CoTs easier to monitor. We first construct a training dataset of paired control--intervention tasks spanning multiple intervention types. Using the dataset, we design and apply three intervention-specific training methods based on off-policy self-distillation, reinforcement learning, and on-policy self-distillation to train separate variants of each base model. Across various model-monitor configurations, all three methods generally improve CoT monitorability over the corresponding base models under our extended evaluation framework. Our code is available at this https URL.

### 3. Incomparable Minima in Agent Tool Authorization

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31914)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`最小权限`、`不可比反链`、`授权理论`

👤 **作者**：Xin Xu

- 🎯 **研究动机**：治理工具授权都要回答“两个都能完成任务的授权哪个更小”——过度特权基准按此打分、权限求解器据此计算
- 🔬 **研究方法**：对照微软与谷歌公开授权表（22305 个 Graph 端点绑定+23852 个 Google API 方法）按各表自身资源结构生成任务，检查最小充分授权是否成反链
- 📌 **结论**：29.8-33.3% 的 Graph 任务与 3.2% 的 Google 资源任务该问题无答案（最小授权构成反链）；微软 privilegeLevel 把 67.4-74.4% 不可比对判等；四种风险度量对 28.7% 不可对排相反方向——单序特权假设失效

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

An agent that needs a tool needs authority to call it, and every proposal for governing that authority has to answer one question: of two grants that both let the agent finish a task, which one is smaller? Over-privilege benchmarks score agents against this answer, and permission solvers compute it. We check the question against the authorisation tables that Microsoft and Google publish, covering 22,305 Microsoft Graph endpoint bindings and 23,852 Google API methods, on tasks generated from each table's own resource structure. For 29.8-33.3% of Graph tasks and 3.2% of Google resource tasks the question has no answer, because the minimal sufficient grants form an antichain, a set of grants none of which authorises a subset of what another does. In its two main authorisation modes, Microsoft's shipped privilegeLevel rates 67.4-74.4% of these incomparable pairs equal. Google ships no such scale, so on Google we apply a reconstruction of the FORTIS privilege ladder, which rates 90.7-98.7% equal. Applying the same reconstruction to Graph attributes much of that difference to the scale rather than the corpus: its form based on the HTTP verb alone rates 92.0% of Google pairs and 89.0-92.0% of Graph pairs equal. Four risk measures (operations, writes, services reached, administrative surface) order 28.7% of incomparable Google pairs and 15.7-23.4% of Graph pairs in opposite directions, so for those pairs the ranking has no measure-independent answer. Offered pools of minimal and larger grants, 6 frontier models pick a minimal grant on 99.0% of 1,708 decisions, a grant smallest under all four measures on 66.7%, and on 22.1% a grant that another option matches or beats on every measure. We propose that benchmarks report which minimal grant an agent chose and that deployments show the alternatives to whoever approves the grant.

</details>

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

### 8. TRACE: Data-Free Text Reconstruction Attacks against Approximate Unlearning in LLMs

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31995)　📅 2026-10

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>
