### 1. Breaking Diffusion: Parallel Reasoning with Draft Refinement

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26542)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`dLLM`、`并行推理`、`草稿精炼`

👤 **作者**：Jaesang Yu、Seoyeon Byun、Yun Wang、Yan Lu、Juho Kim

- 🎯 **研究动机**：扩散去噪的重复并行修订不保证各自合理的词元更新组合成更好的答案
- 🔬 **研究方法**：DRLM 用单一去噪器提出并行修订并评估候选草稿：从均匀损坏草稿重建每个词元学习保留与修复，用已知损坏过程校正自词元证据得到腔能量（单次前向），每轮仅在能量更低时保留修订
- 📌 **结论**：Countdown/Sudoku-Extreme/Maze-Hard 上超自回归与离散扩散基线，6M 参数即达 Countdown-5 44.4%、Sudoku-Extreme 93.9%——完整草稿并行精炼成为反向扩散的有效替代

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Drafting an answer allows a reasoning model to reconsider each decision in the context of the others. Yet repeated diffusion denoising does not ensure that individually plausible token updates form a better answer together. We introduce Draft Refinement Language Models (DRLMs), which use a single denoiser to propose parallel revisions and evaluate a candidate draft. We train the denoiser to reconstruct every token from uniformly corrupted drafts, teaching both preservation and repair. Its predictions specify revisions directly, without a diffusion reverse transition. Evaluation requires a different use of these predictions: confidence in an existing token reflects both contextual support and evidence from observing the token itself. Using the known corruption process, we correct for this self-token evidence to estimate how well each token is supported by the prompt and the remaining draft. Aggregating these estimates yields a cavity energy computed in one forward pass, without an additional scoring model. Each round proposes a revision, reads the completed candidate in its revised context, and retains it only if its energy is lower. Across Countdown, Sudoku-Extreme, and Maze-Hard, our method outperforms autoregressive and discrete diffusion baselines. With 6M parameters, DRLM reaches 44.4% on Countdown-5 and 93.9% on Sudoku-Extreme. The results establish complete-draft parallel refinement as an effective alternative to reverse diffusion for complex reasoning.

</details>

### 2. SimHack: Predicting the onset of detectable reward hacking before RL training

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28707)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`奖励作弊预测`、`RLVR`、`课程筛选`

👤 **作者**：Junwei Deng、Alex Bie、Natalia Ponomareva

- 🎯 **研究动机**：RL 训代码模型的奖励作弊可检测却难预防：环境加固随复杂度愈发困难，训练时干预又激励模型混淆作弊行为以躲避检测
- 🔬 **研究方法**：SimHack 从少量小规模参考 RL 运行学习奖励作弊训练动力学，生成未见目标配方的反事实模拟，预测可检测作弊变为主导的起始步分布
- 📌 **结论**：未见配方上起始步中位数相对误差 11.3%、跨配方秩相关 0.85，泛化到模型规模/课程/rollout 数/学习率差异；筛选出同场景集上多训 96.6% 而不出作弊的课程

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

When training LLMs to code with reinforcement learning, reward hacking is often detectable but difficult to prevent, since (a) environment hardening is increasingly difficult as training environments grow more complex; and (b) training-time interventions incentivize models to obfuscate hacking behavior to bypass detection. We instead study whether we can predict when detectable reward hacking becomes dominant before executing an RL run, enabling us to select lower-risk recipes in advance. We find that the initial hacking rate alone does not determine onset, which varies substantially across training recipes and repeated runs. We thus formulate this problem as predicting the distribution of the reward hacking onset step under a target training recipe. We introduce SimHack, a simulator that learns reward hacking training dynamics from a handful of smaller-scale reference RL runs and generates counterfactual simulation runs of unseen target recipes without costly retraining. Across unseen target recipes, SimHack predicts the median onset to a relative error of 11.3% and achieves across-recipe rank correlation of 0.85, while generalizing to differences in model scale, curriculum, number of rollouts, and learning rate between reference and target recipes. As an application, we show that SimHack can guide training decisions by screening candidate RL curricula, discovering curricula over the same set of scenarios that train for 96.6% longer without hacking.

<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

### 6. PerMIT: Securing Persistent Memory for LLM Agents via Machine-Checked Integrity Theorems and Scalable Community Trust

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28987)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`agent 记忆安全`、`完整性定理`、`社区信任`

👤 **作者**：Tanqiu Jiang、Min Bai、Sadat Shahriar、Wanyu Du、Sandesh Swamy

- 🎯 **研究动机**：持久记忆是基本无防御的注入与投毒面，同一风险也限制 agent 间记忆共享
- 🔬 **研究方法**：PerMIT 在显式信任假设（仅用户输入与门确认值可信）下经有限记忆接口中介环境派生值的释放，两个完整性定理经 Lean 4 机器检查：环境值仅在门确认时到达敏感动作且仅经门显示影响行为；跨用户背书积累成社区确认
- 📌 **结论**：Trojan Hippo 上自验证与轻量验证器下零攻击成功；社区共扩展性质（仿真佐证）：社区越大提升毒物与误逐风险越降而单用户验证负担不升

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Persistent memory is a largely undefended surface for injection and poisoning, and the same risk limits memory sharing among agents. We present PerMIT, a memory architecture with provable integrity guarantees under an explicit trust assumption: only user-typed inputs and values confirmed at a gate are trusted. PerMIT mediates the release and use of untrusted environment-derived values through a finite memory interface, while preserving agent-driven multi-turn planning and tool choice. Over this finite interface, we prove two integrity theorems that are also machine-checked in Lean 4, which together ensure that environment-derived memory reaches a sensitive action only if confirmed at the gate and influences the agent's behavior only through what the gate displays. Under explicit assumptions, we prove and corroborate in simulation a co-scaling property: as the community grows, the risks of promoting poison and of wrongly evicting benign knowledge fall, while per-user verification effort does not rise. On $\tau^2$-bench, communities of up to 90 autonomous agents use PerMIT to build shared knowledge bases with no direct memory injection promoted, and the resulting knowledge significantly improves new agents on held-out tasks, suggesting a path toward agents that safely self-improve at test time.

### 7. Attention-based Classification for Indirect Prompt Injection

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28988)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`间接提示注入检测`、`注意力分析`、`评测纠偏`

👤 **作者**：Amelia Kawasaki、Stefan Lee

- 🎯 **研究动机**：Attention Tracker 宣称用标量专注分数阈值做数据集无关的注入检测，但在真实 agent IPI 环境未经验证
- 🔬 **研究方法**：在 AgentDojo 上首评：长结构化工具输出颠覆其对注意力分布的基本假设；提出 AttnDA 把标量阈值换成逐头专注分数向量上的 Fisher LDA，跨四基准做跨数据集评测
- 📌 **结论**：AgentDojo 上原方法多数模型族近乎随机；AttnDA 提升 agent 与工具集成基准上的检测，但向 AgentDojo 的迁移对任何分类器仍困难，逐头相关结构贡献显著

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Indirect prompt injection (IPI) attacks, in which adversarial instructions are embedded in external data processed by a language model, pose a significant threat to the integrity of deployed LLM agents. Attention Tracker (Hung et al., 2025) is a recently proposed white-box detection method that identifies prompt injection attacks by monitoring how a model's attention is distributed between user task tokens and data tokens, claiming dataset-agnostic detection via a scalar focus score threshold. In this paper, we provide the first evaluation of Attention Tracker in a realistic agentic IPI setting using the AgentDojo benchmark, finding that the dataset agnosticism claim does not hold. A covariance ablation further shows that removing inter-head correlation structure substantially contributes to performance, with complex covariance assumptions causing significant degradation on cross-domain data. These results indicate that the attentional distraction signal underlying focus-score-based detection does not transfer reliably to agentic environments, and that learned linear classifiers over head-level attention features offer improved detection performance, provided training data from the target deployment domain is available.

### 9. Better Policies, Worse Verifier Audits

📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29029)　📅 2026-10

**关键词**：`验证器审计`、`在线策略审计坍塌`、`组件评测`

👤 **作者**：Daniel Cieślak、Jacek Łubiński

- 🎯 **研究动机**：验证器栈日益嵌入后训练与测试时计算回路：组件验证器在新策略输出上对全成功标签评分、聚合分下降即刷新——该控制回路可系统性选错验证器
- 🔬 **研究方法**：对合取目标推导在策 AUC 公式：策略掌握 F 后 q 趋 1 使审计权重移离 F 失败、偏好 M 信息（on-policy audit collapse）；给出尖锐可识别集、完全抹除阈值与偏好更差组件验证器的精确条件，ECG 预测注册研究验证
- 📌 **结论**：按审计行动可降低离策 F-AUC、把损伤转移到未见生成器并增加下游约束违反；修复=在组件自身失败上审计并保持刷新分布中含这些失败

<details>
<summary>📝 展开完整英文摘要（Abstract）</summary>

Verifier stacks increasingly sit inside post-training and test-time compute loops. We study a specific control loop: a component verifier is evaluated on fresh policy outputs against the full success label and refreshed when that aggregate score falls. For a conjunctive target $y = F \wedge M$, its on-policy AUC is $(1-q)A_{\mathrm{own}} + qA_{\mathrm{cross}}$, where $q = \Pr[F=1 \mid y=0]$. As the policy masters $F$, $q$ can rise toward one, removing weight from $F$-failures and rewarding information about $M$. We call this on-policy audit collapse. We derive a sharp identified set, a complete-erasure threshold, and the exact point at which the audit can prefer a worse component verifier; its population optimum is the full-outcome predictor, not the component oracle. An exact oracle exhibits the collapse, a natural multi-label text control shows inversion can select a head with lower canonical $F$-AUC at the observed failure mixture, and a prediction-registered ECG study shows that acting on the audit can reduce off-policy $F$-AUC, transfer the damage to an unseen generator, and increase downstream constraint violations. Preserving own-failure support prevents the damage. Matching label-complexity bounds and a controlled-mixture audit give the corresponding repair: audit the component on its own failures, and keep those failures in the refresh distribution.

</details>
